# BolusBuddy food figures checked against CoFID 2021

## Summary

- I checked all 105 common foods in the app against McCance and Widdowson's CoFID 2021 and the CoFID old foods file.
- 101 foods now use a CoFID entry. Four are not in CoFID and keep their previous value: quinoa (cooked), oat drink, glucose tablets and plain meat, fish or eggs.
- 28 foods changed by 10% or more. The largest are roast parsnips (13 g to 26.6 g), cooked couscous (23 g to 34.2 g) and boiled egg noodles (25 g to 32.5 g).
- All figures are converted to the UK packet-label basis, as you chose. CoFID states carbohydrate as monosaccharide equivalents, so I divided starch by 1.10 and sucrose, maltose and lactose by 1.05 using each food's own breakdown. White sugar goes from CoFID's 105 g to 100 g, which matches the packet.
- Method "exact" means the full sugar breakdown was available. "Approx" means only total sugars were available, so sugars were divided by 1.025. For 114 of the added CoFID foods there is no starch or sugar breakdown, so the total is divided by 1.07. The app marks these as "estimated".
- The app also now includes every CoFID food that has a carb figure: 3,128 more foods, searchable and grouped. 50 CoFID foods have no carbohydrate figure and are left out.
